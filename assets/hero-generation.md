# Hero görselleri

Built-in image_gen ile kullanıcının iki hero referansı, Fiat Ducato fotoğrafı ve Prestij kaplama tasarımı kullanılarak üretildi. Kaynak fotoğraflar değiştirilmedi.

## assets/hero-coast-fiat.png

Use case: compositing. Create a production website hero BACKGROUND PHOTO, landscape 16:9 at high resolution. Input 1 is the hero layout and coastal sunset photographic reference; input 2 is the exact vehicle identity to insert, a white Fiat Ducato light commercial box van with over-cab tall white cargo body, small wheels, Fiat front, license 45 AGJ 350; input 3 is the exact black gold white Prestij cargo wrap artwork. Replace the reference's large Mercedes truck with this Fiat Ducato van. No Mercedes truck. Apply artwork 3 as a full black panel on the entire visible side cargo curtain, perspective fitted within all four corners, fully inside its frame, keep curtain fasteners and rails visible. Preserve readable PRESTİJ NAKLİYAT, EVDEN EVE VE MOBİLYA MONTAJ, ZONGULDAK branding from artwork. Do not stretch outside body. White cab, authentic compact van proportions. Reorient van to face toward viewer/right like hero reference so broad branded cargo side visible. Keep vehicle entirely in RIGHT half of image, occupying x=52%-96%, y=25%-88%, all wheels visible. LEFT 48% should be dark almost black negative space for LIVE HTML headline. Remove ALL reference's website logo, headlines, pills, buttons, benefit icons, browser frame, UI and any typography outside the actual van wrap. This is ONLY a cinematic coastal sunset road photograph, not a screenshot. Match warm amber lighting, dramatic black contrast, seafront hillside homes and sunset sky from reference. Ground vehicle naturally with shadows and road reflections. No extra vehicles. Scene 1: parked on coastal overlook, warm sunset sea and town, wet dark road, rich golden sunlight on right.
